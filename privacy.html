Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

IEP Binder does not collect any information about you or your child.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter stays on your device. That includes names, dates, notes, your contact log, goals, meeting notes and scanned papers. We cannot see it. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

What we do not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No ads.
  • No tracking or analytics.
  • No artificial intelligence services. Scanned text is read on your device.

Camera and photos

The app uses the camera only when you choose to scan a paper. It reads photos or files only when you pick them. Scans are saved on your device.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, your phone schedules them itself. Nothing is sent over the internet.

Backups

If you back up your phone with iCloud or a computer, your binder is included in that backup. Apple's own privacy policy covers those backups.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is handled by Apple. We do not receive your name, email or payment details.

Sharing and export

When you export a PDF, you choose where it goes. The app sends nothing by itself.

Links

The Guide has a link to find your state's Parent Center. It opens in your browser, and that website has its own privacy policy.

Deleting your information

You can delete any item, or a child's whole binder, inside the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.

Children

IEP Binder is made for parents and guardians. It is not meant to be used by children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
