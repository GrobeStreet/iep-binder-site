Help

Short answers to common questions.

Where is my information kept?

Only on your phone. There is no account and nothing is sent to us.

What happens after the 21-day free trial?

You can still read and export everything, for free, forever. To keep adding new items, you pay once. There is no subscription.

I paid before. How do I unlock a new phone?

Open Settings in the app (the gear on the Home screen) and tap Restore a purchase. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I move my binder to a new phone?

Back up your old phone and restore the backup to the new one. Your binder comes with it. You can also export each child's binder as a PDF from Settings.

How do I add a paper?

Open Binder, tap the plus button, and give the paper a name. You can scan it with the camera, choose photos, or import a PDF.

Why can't I find a word I searched for?

Search reads the printed words on scanned pages. Handwriting and blurry scans may not be found. Try scanning again in good light.

The dates are different in my state.

The app shows the federal deadlines. Some states give schools less time. You can add your own dates under Key dates, Edit.

Is this legal advice?

No. IEP Binder gives general information. For advice about your situation, contact your state's Parent Center or a special education attorney.

How do I delete everything?

In Settings, tap a child, then Delete this child. Deleting the app removes everything it stored on that device.
